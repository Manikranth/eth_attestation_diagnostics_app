▸ what does each column mean? (plain-English legend)

INFORMATION — what happened

on chain
did a block actually land at your duty slot
head / tgt / src
were your head, target and source votes correct. – means unknown — the node was still syncing, so we don't guess (this is the fix for the old false ✓).
head lag
how many slots behind your duty slot the block you voted for was. 0 is ideal.
incl slot / dist
which slot included your vote, and how far after your duty slot that was (1 = ideal).
missed
your attestation never made it on chain.
head@tick / behind / sync
your node's health at the slot: its head slot, how far behind, and sync state.

ATTESTATION — the pipeline, in the order it happened

Each column is tagged BN (beacon node), EL (execution layer) or VC (validator client). Columns marked as an offset are "how many ms after slot start" — they overlap and do not add up. Only the STAGES (Δ) columns are sequential and sum.

seen
block first seen + verified on gossip (offset).
late by
how late Lighthouse judged the block (duration).
blob src
where the blobs came from: gossip, el (getBlobs from execution), gossip+el, or none.
getBlobs req / blobs EL / cols EL
getBlobs request offset, blobs served/expected by the EL, and columns already held via the EL.
data ready
all blob/column data available (offset).
consensus / EL verify
how long consensus and execution-layer verification each took (durations — only logged on late blocks).
imported / attestable
block imported into fork choice, and all data attestable — the moment the beacon node was done and ready for the VC (offsets).
st:propag / st:blobs / st:import
the sequential stage gaps — these DO sum to the total.
att start / sign+pub / broadcast
VC started producing, how long produce+sign+publish took, and when the vote hit the wire (offset). broadcast is the single end-to-end number.

AGGREGATION — did an aggregator carry your vote

in agg
your vote was included in an on-chain aggregate.
picked
an aggregator picked up your vote on gossip (live p2p watcher).
agg bits
how many validators were in the aggregate that carried your vote / committee size.
agg pub
when our node published its aggregate (only if this validator was an aggregator).
peers
node-wide peer count at the slot (a proxy — not a true per-aggregation count).
Δ blk
n/a — the delta between the block the aggregator saw and the block you attested needs a p2p sentry (deferred to a later phase).

PROPAGATION — how your vote spread

propag
time from slot start until your vote first hit the wire (live p2p watcher).
subnet
the gossip subnet your attestation was published on.
seen by
n/a — which peer / remote attester first saw your vote needs a p2p sentry (deferred).

VERDICT

fault
one-word classification of how the slot went (perfect, included_late, wrong_head_vote, block_arrived_late, …) and, when missed, the most likely reason.
